## Question 1


## 涉及卡牌

- Virulent Emissary {G} Creature &#8211; Elf Assassin 1/1 Deathtouch Whenever another creature you control enters, you gain 1 life.
- Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice {2}{B} Creature &#8211; Orc Warlock 2/2 Infusion &#8211; When this creature enters, target creature an opponent controls gets -4/-4 until end of turn if you gained life this turn.
- Hazel&#8217;s Nocturne {3}{B} Instant Return up to two target creature cards from your graveyard to your hand. Each opponent loses 2 life and you gain 2 life.

## 题目

Astrid controls Virulent Emissary and her life total has not changed yet this turn. Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice enters the battlefield under Astrid’s control and she puts both enters-the-battlefield abilities onto the stack, with Virulent Emissary’s on top. Napoleon responds with Hazel&#8217;s Nocturne . Which answer best describes what happens when the ability of Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice resolves?

## 选项

A. If Napoleon responds at any time before the ability of Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice resolves, that ability does not resolve.
B. If Napoleon responds before Virulent Emissary’s ability resolves, the ability of Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice resolves and the target creature gets -4/-4 until end of turn. If he responds after Virulent Emissary’s ability resolves and before the ability of Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice resolves, that ability resolves but the target creature does not get -4/-4 until end of turn.
C. The ability of Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice resolves and the target creature gets -4/-4 until end of turn.
D. This can&#8217;t happen as described. The ability of Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice does not trigger when it enters the battlefield.
E. If Napoleon responds at any time before the ability of Poisoner&#8217;s Apprentice resolves, that ability resolves but the target creature does not get -4/-4 until end of turn.

## Question 2


## 涉及卡牌

- Living End Sorcery Suspend 3-{2}{B}{B} Each player exiles all creature cards from their graveyard, then sacrifices all creatures they control, then puts all cards they exiled this way onto the battlefield.

- Biblioplex Tomekeeper {4} Artifact Creature &#8211; Construct 3/4 When this creature enters, choose up to one &#8211; * Target creature becomes prepared. (Only creatures with prepare spells can become prepared.) * Target creature becomes unprepared.

## 题目

During his main phase, Abel resolves Living End and puts Cheerful Osteomancer onto the battlefield. Nina puts Biblioplex Tomekeeper onto the battlefield. Which answer best describes what happens, assuming that Nina targets Cheerful Osteomancer with the second mode of Biblioplex Tomekeeper’s ability?

## 选项

A. Abel puts Cheerful Osteomancer’s prepare triggered ability onto the stack, then Nina puts Biblioplex Tomekeeper’s ability onto the stack. The prepare triggered ability resolves last, Cheerful Osteomancer becomes prepared, and Abel creates a copy of Raise Dead, which he may cast immediately after.
B. Abel creates a copy of Raise Dead simultaneously with Cheerful Osteomancer entering the battlefield, and has one opportunity to cast it before Nina puts Biblioplex Tomekeeper’s ability onto the stack.
C. Cheerful Osteomancer enters the battlefield prepared. Abel puts its prepare triggered ability onto the stack, then Nina puts Biblioplex Tomekeeper’s ability onto the stack. When the latter resolves, Cheerful Osteomancer becomes unprepared. Then the prepare triggered ability resolves and Abel does not create a copy of Raise Dead.
D. Abel creates a copy of Raise Dead simultaneously with Cheerful Osteomancer entering the battlefield, but he cannot cast it before Biblioplex Tomekeeper’s ability resolves.
